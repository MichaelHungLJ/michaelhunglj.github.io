Coming into 2026, I wanted to look back at my life in 2025. It was a difficult year. <i>I had many doubts about myself.</i>
</div>

&nbsp;

### 2025 Struggles

During Q1, I was mostly coasting and felt like I was wasting my life away. Sure, I was working and clocking in and out, but it didn’t feel like I was making any real progress.

Going into Q2, it was honestly one of the toughest quarters of the year. I was burned out from work and deeply frustrated with myself. I had made a promise to myself long ago, yet I found myself drifting aimlessly again.

I felt lost and worried, despite knowing I was in a privileged position. I needed isolation to get away from all the noise. Sometimes, I looked at other people and wondered how could they live knowing the ground they stood on could crumble any time. I know I shouldn’t compare myself to others, but that was how I felt.

<i>Even in a privileged position, I know it wouldn’t last forever.</i> I needed to find my footing and fast. But between the burnout, the constant noise, and the uncertainty of what to do next. I didn't want to talk to anyone. I just wanted to get away from the noise and find some space to reflect.

---
&nbsp;

In Q3, I decided to be harsher with myself. No more bullshit, only focus, even if it hurt. 

<div align="center"> <b>No excuses</b>. </div>

<div align = "center">I forced myself to go to the gym even when I was tired.</div>
<div align = "center">I forced myself to eat more even when I was full.</div>
<div align = "center">I prioritised my own time over family.</div>
&nbsp;

Q3 honestly felt like dread. Time moved painfully slowly, yet my mind was in a constant state of focus. So focused on not giving myself any excuses. Every day felt like a week.

I did break some bad habits: Doom scrolling, Procrastination, Sleeping Late. But even then, it didn’t feel like growth or progress. 

<b>Every step feels heavy. There was no road to walk on. No path ahead. All fog. I was walking in blind faith, hoping it's the right path, hoping to see something.</b>

I thought setting goals would help me understand myself, my life goals, what I actually wanted. But it didn’t.

---
&nbsp;

In Q4, it was mostly the holiday period. I went on a fairly long trip before COVID hit. A week of fever pretty much wrecked my momentum, my health and my weight goal in particular.

I decided to remote work in Taiwan, hoping to find my life’s purpose (人生的路). I didn’t find it. But being around friends, eating well, exploring, and spending the night just chatting and chill drinking gave me some clarity about my struggles.

More importantly, when I look back, I'd realised how harsh I was to myself and impatient I was.

Maybe I didn’t need to be so harsh on myself.<br>
Maybe I didn’t need to have control over everything in my life.<br>
Things I can change, I change. Things I can’t, I accept.<br>

I have yet to set up my 2026 goals but there are few things I'm sure I know 

I want to be kinder and kinder to myself <br>
I want to be patient <br>
I want to be more clear on what I want <br>
I want to explore more <br>
I want to learn to live a little and laugh more <br>
